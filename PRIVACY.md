# Dr Jay Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Dr Jay is developed by Jeyanth Mandava. This policy covers the iPhone app, its widgets, Live Activities, and optional on-device models.

## Your entries stay local

Dr Jay has no account system, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics service. Sleep and water logs, food descriptions and assessments, exercise entries, learned food corrections, check-in messages, and settings are stored on your device. The app and widgets share local storage so logging actions and displayed progress agree.

Your entries are not uploaded to the developer or sent to a remote AI service. Apple Foundation Models and the optional Gemma model process text on your device. Generated daily-report and insights commentary is not saved; sleep, water, and food feedback associated with logged entries may be saved in history.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Dr Jay reads sleep information and today's step count from Apple Health. It does not write to Apple Health. Sleep information used in your daily record can be stored locally and included in a JSON export. Step totals are displayed and used for the current daily report, but are not saved in Dr Jay's history or exports. Insights do not retain historical step counts.

You can stop sleep syncing in Settings. To revoke Health permissions, use the Health app's access controls for Dr Jay. Turning off access does not delete previously saved sleep entries; use Reset App Data to erase them. Dr Jay is an accountability and wellbeing tool, not medical care, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Brain Dump

Brain Dump messages and responses exist only in memory for the open conversation. Closing the pane discards them. They are not saved to history, logged by Dr Jay, exported, or used to train a model. The pane obscures its contents when the app becomes inactive. This is not a guarantee against screenshots you take or records created outside the app.

## Downloads and external links

If you choose Gemma, Dr Jay downloads the model from Hugging Face and its delivery providers. The request transmits ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and requested file, to those services. It does not include your health records or Brain Dump text. The downloaded model, temporary download files, and local model cache are stored separately from your entries and excluded from device backups where controlled by the app.

Opening the public policy, source repository, or other external links connects to the relevant website under that site's privacy practices. No personal entries are attached to those links.

## Backups and deletion

Dr Jay maintains a local JSON backup of logged history and learned food corrections. It is not encrypted by the app. The private health-data container and the automatic JSON backup are excluded from system cloud backups. The app does not provide cloud sync.

A backup leaves the app only when you explicitly export or share it. If you save that export to iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider handles your copy. Protect exported files because they contain personal information. Brain Dump conversations, historical step totals, model files, and generated report or insights commentary are not included in the JSON backup.

Settings → Reset App Data deletes Dr Jay's stored history, learned corrections, settings, automatic JSON backup, and optional Gemma installation. This does not erase Apple Health's records or backup files you previously exported. Delete those exported copies separately wherever you saved them.

## Notifications and diagnostics

Notifications, widgets, and Live Activities can display your progress or commentary outside the app. Manage notification previews and Lock Screen visibility in iOS Settings if you prefer to keep them private. Dr Jay keeps local operational error logs; it does not intentionally log personal entry text, and error details are marked private.

Apple may collect diagnostics according to your device settings. TestFlight also provides Apple and the developer with beta crash reports and feedback under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms. Feedback, screenshots, and emails you send may contain personal data, so avoid including sensitive entries. Brain Dump is not a feedback collection channel.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [Dr Jay repository](https://github.com/JayMandava/dr-jay). Do not post health information or Brain Dump text in public issues. A direct privacy contact will be added before external beta distribution. Future policy changes will update the effective date and the policy bundled with the app.
